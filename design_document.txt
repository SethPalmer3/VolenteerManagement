------------ Volunteer Management System ---------------
Overview:
	Implement a new system that helps staff coordinate volunteers in events/tasks. This system will use a post-board signup type system to achive this.
Staff will be able to achive this by posting a event/task that needs to get done with roles that that event/task will require. Once posted volunteers will
get emails with the details of this event IF there is a role or multiple roles that they choose to sign up for. Volunteers will choose to accept the invitation
for a specific role(possibility of volunteers accepting more than one role). Staff may choose to deny a volunteer for their event if either the volunteer changes
their acceptance or feel they are unfit for such a task(needs to be trained on a specific task .etc). Each event will keep track of how many people in each role 
is needed and keep track of how many people are left to fill a position. Once the event is over, staff indicate who did actually show up and for how long(details
about this may change) if that information is needed in the future(volunteer tracking hours, keeping volunteers and staff accountable, .etc). Once all data is set
or if the edit periode is up, the event data will be locked down and can no longer be edited except for administrators(not staff necessarily). 
Goals:
	- Make scheduling volunteers and mananging their time quick and efficient
	- Make these events/task as visible to as many volunteers as possible and easy for them to take on these roles
	- View past events data inuitive and useful
Data:
	Staff - Sets up events(time, date, type, .etc). They also set up the roles that are needed.
		They can send special invites to specific volunteers with automatic acceptance. They approve which of the volunteers are accepted into a role.
	Volunteer - Someone who "applys" for events specified by a staff memeber. They indicate what kinds of roles they want to participate in and will
		get emailed about events with roles they want to participate in. They can indicate whether they accept or not.
	Events - Specific instances where help is needed. These can be actual events or just "jobs" that need to get done.
		- Time and date
		- Place
		- Est. Duration
		- Set of roles with the current number of volunteers needed in that role ({PetCleaner: 2, QuestSignIn: 0, ...}). These numbers will get
		deducted as volunteers accept these positions.
		- Lockout; used for when the event has passed and this event can no longer be edited.
	Roles - The types of jobs that volunteers could be doing on any given event(This is a WIP list)
		- AnimalLivingAreaCleaner
		- NonMedicalAnimalCare
		- MedicalAnimalCare
		- EventGuestServices
		- EventSetup
		- EventTearDown
		- EventBooth
		- StaffHelp
